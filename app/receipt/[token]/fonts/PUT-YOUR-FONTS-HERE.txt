Put a copy of your two brand font files in this folder, named exactly:

  display.otf   ← La Luxes Serif   (used for the big amount)
  body.otf      ← Recoleta         (used for all other text)

If your files end in .ttf or .woff instead, keep that ending:
  display.ttf / body.ttf   or   display.woff / body.woff

.woff2 files do NOT work for share pictures. If you only have .woff2,
use the .otf or .ttf from where you bought/downloaded the font.

You can delete this text file after adding the fonts.
