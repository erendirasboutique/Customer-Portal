The receipt share picture already uses the brand fonts in /public/fonts/:

  TAN-Nightingale-Regular.otf  (the big amount and "Tu recibo")
  Edinburgh-Regular.otf        (all other text)

Only add files here if you want to override those, named exactly:

  display.otf   <- heading font
  body.otf      <- body font

.woff2 files do NOT work for share pictures.
